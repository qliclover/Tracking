隐私政策

有余 · Margin　最后更新:2026 年 7 月

有余是一个极简的月度预算记账工具。我们的原则很简单:你的数据首先是你自己的,能留在你手机本地就不上传。

我们存储什么

你的预算、账目、固定账单、分类、个人资料(昵称/头像)默认只保存在你手机的本地存储中,不会发送到任何服务器。

拍照记账 / 语音记账

只有当你主动点击"拍照"或"语音"功能时,对应的小票照片或语音转写文字才会发送给 AI 服务(通义千问 / 阿里云百炼)用于识别金额、商家和分类。这些内容仅用于当次识别,不会被用来做其他用途,也不会关联到你的身份。

相机与麦克风权限

App 会请求相机权限用于拍摄小票、请求麦克风权限用于语音记账。这两个权限仅在你主动使用对应功能时才会被访问,你可以随时在系统设置中关闭。

云同步(可选)

如果你在设置中主动配置了云同步(LeanCloud),你的记账数据会加密同步到你自己配置的 LeanCloud 应用中,用于多设备同步。不配置的情况下,数据完全不会离开你的设备。

我们不做的事

  • 不接入任何第三方广告 SDK
  • 不接入任何用户行为分析 / 埋点服务
  • 不会出售或共享你的数据给第三方
  • 不要求注册账号

数据删除

你可以随时在"设置 → 数据"中导出备份或清空所有数据。卸载 App 也会清除本地保存的全部数据。

联系我们

如果你对隐私政策有任何疑问,可以通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们,或发邮件至 clover19990306@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Margin　Last updated: July 2026

Margin is a minimalist monthly-budget tracker. Our principle is simple: your data is yours first — it stays on your device unless you explicitly choose otherwise.

What we store

Your budget, expenses, fixed bills, categories, and profile (name/avatar) are stored only in your device's local storage by default — nothing is sent to any server.

Receipt scanning / voice entry

Only when you tap "Scan" or "Speak" is the receipt photo or voice transcript sent to an AI service (Qwen / Alibaba Cloud DashScope) to extract the amount, merchant, and category. This content is used only for that one recognition request and is not linked to your identity.

Camera & microphone

The app requests camera access to photograph receipts and microphone access for voice entry. Both are only accessed when you actively use those features, and you can revoke either permission anytime in system settings.

Cloud sync (optional)

If you configure cloud sync (LeanCloud) in Settings, your data syncs to your own LeanCloud app for multi-device use. Without configuration, nothing ever leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No third-party ad SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking services
  • We never sell or share your data
  • No account registration required

Deleting your data

Export a backup or clear all data anytime from Settings → Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.
